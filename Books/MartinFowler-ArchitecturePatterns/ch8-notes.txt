Putting it all together
	Three things that will make changing your mind easier: CI, TDD, refactoring

	1. Choose domain layer approach, probably Domain Model, for which:
		Pros: OOP!
		Cons: hard to do well, not obvious how to map to a relational db

	2. Data Source layer (based on the domain decision). For DM:
		Pretty simple & close to db  -> Active Record
		Want to decouple? -> use Gateways (Probably Row)
		Complicated -> Data Mapper + Unit of Work

	3. Pesentation layer:
		Page Controller + Template View (???)

	Other layering schemes. 
	Brown Layers: Data source, Data mapping, Domain, Controller, Presentation
	Marinescu layers: Data source, Domain, Services(Use-Cases), Application, Presentation
